## Two research-grade findings

### Finding 1 — Sajja P1 autocorr is reachable for row-aware generators (B3)

Sajja's Proposition 2 says row-independent generators cannot produce
positive within-entity IET autocorrelation. The paper's row-independent
baselines land at **25.9×** (TVAE post-conditional) to **30.0×** (CTGAN)
on this metric.

**DataSynth B3 lands at 20.7× — below TVAE-paper level.** The mechanism
isn't burst clustering at the IET level (that path was dead-code under
the production config — see Finding 2). It's **temporal density
redistribution via per-process fraud rates**: uneven fraud-rate
distribution across processes creates denser intra-process fraud
temporal sequences, lifting within-source lag-1 autocorr in the fraud
cohort.

Trade-off: P3 fanout regresses 382→771× because uneven fraud reshapes
TP usage via the SOTA-11.1-capped 12-TP pool. Two follow-ups close it:
(a) raise the TP-pool cap toward reference's 36, (b) tune per-process
rate spread to smaller deltas.

For the v5.30 SOTA release, the trade-off makes B3 an **opt-in research
lever** — schema feature in mainline, config block commented out.

`docs/baselines/2026-05-26-v5.30-b3-per-process-fraud/COMPARISON.md`

### Finding 2 — SP3 priors aren't loaded under the production config (B1)

The `industry_profile: manufacturing` bare-string form in
`configs/examples/hf/journal_entries_1m_sota.yaml` returns `None` from
`IndustryProfileField::priors()`. The orchestrator's priors-loading
block at `enhanced_orchestrator.rs:11181` is therefore **skipped**.

Downstream consequence: the entire SP3 IET sampling block at
`je_generator.rs:2127-2207` is **dead code in every regen this session
ran** — including all the v5.27/v5.28/v5.29 regens that ship as HF
datasets. The B1 patches (Phase 1 source-keying, Phase 2 burst
clustering) target that dead path; both are correctness-preserving but
have zero observable output impact today.

A3's TAIL_MASS shift worked anyway because `sample_sap_source_code()`
has a separate fallback path through a `LazyLock` of
`SourceMixPrior::sap_default()`. That path bypasses the priors-loading
gate and uses the modified constant unconditionally.

The Sajja P1 autocorr DR of 62.84× (post-A3, pre-B3) **doesn't come from
the SP3 IET sampler at all**. The synth's 0.0225 within-source autocorr
comes from the temporal_sampler's seasonality / holiday / business-day
patterns leaking structural clustering into the day-resolution
timestamps.

Three forward paths (deferred):
1. Refactor IET burst logic onto the temporal_sampler path (~200 LOC,
   touches a hot path)
2. Build a manufacturing.dsf priors bundle + opt the config into the
   full-struct form (1-2 weeks data work)
3. Document the priors-gating semantics in `CLAUDE.md` and the SP3
   design doc to prevent future re-discovery
